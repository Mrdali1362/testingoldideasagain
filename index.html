How it works

Two minutes. No camera.

  1. 1
    Add your roomsPick each room and roughly how big it is.
  2. 2
    Sound checkStand in each room for 5 seconds. Your phone plays a short tone and listens to the echo.
  3. 3
    See your setupSpeakers matched to each room, all playing together.
Nothing is recorded. The echo is measured on your phone and turned into one number per room. No images, no audio leaves your device.
